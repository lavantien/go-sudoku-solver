# evolve run notes

Fixture origin: `SampleExtreme81` and `SampleExtremeSolution81` in `internal/puzzle/fixtures.go` were evolved on 2026-10-19 by `tools/evolve`, run with `make evolve`. The tool is deterministic: every parameter is a fixed constant, the PCG stream is seeded with `(20261019, 20261019)`, fitness is the step count of `solver.Propagate` under `config.Defaults`, and the run stopped on its generation budget, so a rerun reproduces the winner byte for byte. The re-pin tests are `TestExtremeSampleIsLegit`, `TestExtremeSampleStepsPinned`, and `TestSamplesList` in `internal/puzzle/fixtures_test.go`.

Run parameters: seed 20261019, population 40 (39 seeds from `puzzle.Generate` cycling clue targets 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 28, 32 with derived seeds, plus the legacy 17-clue sample topped to 20 clues with lowest-index solution digits), 400 generations, elitism 2, tournament k 3, clue band 20 to 40, crossover 10 percent, mutation draws relabel 25, swap 30, remove 35, add 10, re-dig budget 12 removal attempts per repair, per-evaluation stall guard 20 s, wall-clock cap 10 min. The committed run stopped by generation budget after 400 generations in 3m17.561s, well under the cap, so machine speed cannot change the outcome.

Fitness progression of the best per generation: 137 at gen 0, 1189 at 40, 1322 at 80, 1330 at 120, 1343 at 160, 1437 at 200, 2649 at 240, 4582 at 280, 4623 at 320 and 360, 4646 at 400. Operator ledger: crossover accepted 134 of 1571, relabel 3493 of 3493, swap 2509 of 4034 with 3850 repairs, remove 3427 of 4775 with 4662 repairs, add 1327 of 1327. Final population: mean steps 2323, min 52, mean clues 23, clue range 21 to 30. Winner: 21 clues, 4646 propagate steps, 72.6 times the classic sample's 64.

Two earlier designs were implemented and replaced, with the evidence in the git history of this commit window. Blind in-band swaps with a uniqueness gate on every operator plateaued at 61 steps in under a second: every `puzzle.Generate` seed solved by singles alone at exactly `81 - clues` steps, relabel is fitness neutral without branch decisions, remove hits the band floor, and only 2 percent of swaps stayed unique. Validity-targeted swaps plus fast evaluation paths reached 311 steps and then froze for 175 generations at the minimality boundary, where about 95 percent of structural moves break uniqueness and the plain rejection of illegitimate children strands the walk on isolated points. The shipped design composes the same operator set into a ruin-and-recreate step: a swap or remove that breaks uniqueness is repaired by the clue-add operator drawing digits from the ruined grid's own engine solution (uniqueness restoring by construction) and re-dug by uniqueness-checked removes back toward the parent's clue count. Relabel and add-from-own-solution skip the `CountSolutions` gate because digit bijections and solution-digit adds preserve uniqueness by construction; swap, remove, and crossover keep the full gate.

Rules carried from the corpus (dsa ch 41, genetic algorithms: encoding and selection, crossover mutation ox): tournament selection as the pressure knob that does not depend on fitness sums, elitism carrying the best individual forward untouched, and a repair operator as the honest reading of a population frozen by selection pressure, per the seed 2 cautionary callout.

Benchmarks live in `internal/solver/bench_test.go` behind `make bench`: propagate runs the extreme sample at about 25.5 ms per op against 143 us for the classic sample, exact cover at 1.85 ms, and anneal and race pay the full local-search budget at about 625 ms.
